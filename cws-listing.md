## Full description (CWS listing body)

**Dyslexia reading chrome. Reading overlay for any web page. Font swap, Irlen color tint, letter/word/line spacing, and a tracking reading ruler — in one calm, private, free tool.**

Bright white pages, cramped lines, and default fonts make the web exhausting for dyslexic, low-vision, and ADHD readers. **Dyslexia Reading Overlay** gives you the four reading aids competitors ship separately — font swap, Irlen-style color tint, letter/word/line spacing, and a tracking reading ruler — in one extension. **Everything OpenDyslexic does — plus tint, spacing, and a tracking ruler.** OpenDyslexic and Lexend are bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License; no CDN, works offline.

**How it works**
- Click the toolbar icon on any tab.
- Turn on the font swap (OpenDyslexic or Lexend), pick an Irlen color tint, and tune letter/word/line spacing to your eye.
- On Pro, switch on the tracking ruler to dim everything above and below the line you're reading.

The overlay is applied on demand into the tab you act on (`activeTab`) — no passive injection, no broad host permission by default. Tint and ruler each live in their own closed shadow DOM with `pointer-events: none`, so they never intercept clicks or break page interaction. Accessibility is the product here, not a bolt-on: WCAG AA contrast, keyboard drivability, focus rings, `prefers-reduced-motion`, and dark mode are core.

**Four reading aids in one**
1. **Dyslexia-friendly fonts** — Switch any page to OpenDyslexic (heavy-bottomed letters) or Lexend (reading-fluency metrics) with one click. Both bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License.
2. **Irlen-style color tint** — A full-viewport overlay (Blue, Yellow, Green, Rose, Slate) via `mix-blend-mode: multiply`. Reduces visual stress on bright white backgrounds without blocking clicks.
3. **Letter / word / line spacing + readable measure** — Tune letter-spacing (0–0.2em), word-spacing (0–0.4em), line-height (1.0–2.4), and max-width (480–1200px) for the optimal 45–75 character line.
4. **Reading ruler (Pro)** — A tracking focus band follows your cursor or arrow keys, dimming everything above and below so your eye locks onto the line you're reading. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Free vs Pro**
- **Free, forever:** OpenDyslexic + Lexend fonts, five Irlen tint presets, all spacing sliders, manual per-page apply. There is no cap on font or tint use.
- **Pro ($99 one-time, yours forever):** Tracking reading ruler, full custom color picker + opacity, auto-apply on every page with per-site profiles, PDF reading mode, and text-to-speech read-aloud.
- Free users get full value on every font, tint, and spacing control. The Pro wall appears only on the first ruler / custom-tint / auto-apply / TTS tap — after the aha.

**Font licensing**
Bundles OpenDyslexic and Lexend under the SIL Open Font License. The OFL license file ships in the package. No CDN, no remote font fetch — both fonts work offline.

**Private by design**
- 100% local in the free tier. No account, no analytics, no telemetry.
- The extension never sends page content anywhere.
- The only outbound request is an optional license check for Pro features, gated behind a user gesture and run on a 12-hour timer — never on your reading path.
- Pro sync uses Chrome's built-in `storage.sync` (operated by Google under your signed-in profile) or Supabase with your license key only — never your browsing data.
- No third-party trackers, no advertising SDKs, no remote configuration.

**Single purpose**
Improve readability of web pages for dyslexic, low-vision, and ADHD readers with font, tint, spacing, and ruler aids.

**Permission justifications**

- **storage** — Remember your font, tint, spacing, and ruler settings.
- **activeTab** — Apply the overlay to the tab you're reading, on your action.
- **scripting** — Inject the overlay, tint layer, and ruler into the page.
- **alarms** — Periodic license re-verification (Pro feature).
- **optional_host_permissions: `["<all_urls>"]`** — Auto-apply the overlay on every page (Pro opt-in feature). Requested at runtime via user gesture. Free tier never requests it.

No `tabs`, no `webRequest`, no `declarativeNetRequest`, no `<all_urls>` in required permissions. No telemetry.

**Remote code**
None. All logic ships in the package.

**Data usage certification**
No personal data collected, transmitted, or sold. The only outbound request is license verification to a Zovo-owned Supabase endpoint, carrying only the license key string. The extension never reads or transmits page content.
